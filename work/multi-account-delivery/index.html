5 Factors I Evaluate
1. Client Type

2. Urgency

Time-Critical

3. Client Expectations

Immediate Turnaround

High visibility; requires active updates.

4. Team Availability

Reallocate Capacity

I shift available developers or designers immediately.

5. Existing Commitments

Reprioritize Queue

Flexible and internal tasks pause temporarily.

How I handle it: I assess who on the team can pivot immediately, adjust timelines on flexible or internal tasks, and keep the Account Manager updated on our progress.
