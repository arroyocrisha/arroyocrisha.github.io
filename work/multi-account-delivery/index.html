Project Overview

At SEO Hacker, digital project management requires orchestrating simultaneous deliverables across a portfolio of 31 client accounts. These accounts range in scope from monthly organic search optimization and technical audits to multi-month website rebuilds and content operations.

My responsibility as Digital Project Manager is to establish operational clarity: ensuring that every specialist—developers, designers, SEO strategists, and account managers—has unambiguous sprint priorities, realistic deadlines, and the resources necessary to deliver without friction.

The Core Challenge

Managing High-Volume Concurrency Without Sacrificing Execution Quality

In a fast-paced agency setting, managing over 30 client accounts concurrently presents three major friction points:

01 / BOTTLENECK RISKS

Resource Contention

Shared developers and designers being pulled in multiple directions when several client milestones hit at the same time.

02 / UNMANAGED SCOPE

Client Revision Churn

Unstructured feedback loops from client teams creating scope drift and delaying scheduled sprint releases.

03 / VISIBILITY GAPS

Stakeholder Alignment

Keeping internal account managers and external clients synchronized on realistic delivery dates and trade-offs.

My Role & Cross-Functional Coordination

I serve as the central coordination hub between client account leads and production squads. I do not replace the specialist expertise of designers or engineers; rather, I create the governance system that enables them to succeed.

The 4-Stage Multi-Account Delivery Workflow

To systematically manage 31 accounts without dropping deliverables, I implemented a 4-stage operational rhythm:

PHASE 01

Workload Triage & Estimation

Evaluate incoming briefs from account managers, validate scope feasibility, and negotiate realistic delivery estimates.

Weekly Capacity Review
PHASE 02

Resource Allocation

Assign tasks across developers, designers, and SEO writers using workload heatmaps to prevent burnout.

Hubstaff & Jira Balancing
PHASE 03

Execution & Blocker Clearing

Run daily check-ins to identify technical blockers, handle scope adjustments, and ensure sprints remain on schedule.

Daily Standups & Risk Audits
PHASE 04

QA & Milestone Closure

Conduct thorough cross-functional verification, verify SEO tags and client revisions, before delivering to the client.

Final Verification Gate

Key Project Decisions & Tradeoffs

1. Standardizing Definition of Ready (DoR) and Done (DoD)

Previously, requests would enter the development queue without complete creative assets or SEO briefs, causing developers to pause mid-task. I established a mandatory "Definition of Ready" checklist: no engineering task is scheduled until wireframes, copy, and tracking requirements are fully approved.

2. Time-Boxed Client Revision Windows

To prevent open-ended revision cycles from delaying other client accounts, I introduced structured revision windows with clear turnaround milestones. This gave clients predictable timelines while safeguarding team bandwidth.
