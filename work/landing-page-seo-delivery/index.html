06 / WHAT I'VE LEARNED

"Landing pages are an important part of the SEO strategy, so their delivery requires attention from every team involved. I've learned that even when the strategy is already established, the work depends on how clearly requirements are passed between teams and how consistently progress is followed through to publication."
