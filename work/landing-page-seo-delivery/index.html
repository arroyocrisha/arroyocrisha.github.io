Landing Page & SEO Project Delivery

Directing the end-to-end delivery of search-optimized landing pages and content architecture, aligning strategic SEO intent with rigorous editorial and technical standards.

My Role Digital Project Manager & Operations Lead
Timeline 3 Months
Team & Stakeholders SEO Strategists, Content Writers, UI Designer & Client Team

Project Overview

High-performing organic growth requires more than generic blog articles. It demands structured landing pages, precise content architecture, and meticulous optimization for both traditional search engines and emerging AI answer engines (AEO/GEO).

In this engagement, I managed the operational execution of a multi-tier content and landing page rollout. My responsibility was translating high-level SEO masterplans into concrete writer briefs, managing review cadences, verifying technical SEO compliance, and ensuring consistent publishing schedules.

The Core Challenge

Balancing Commercial Brand Messaging with Strict Search Engine Optimization Intent

Content-driven digital delivery often faces three common points of breakdown:

01 / BRAND VS SEARCH

Intent Alignment

Harmonizing brand positioning guidelines with exact search intent and AI answer snippet requirements.

02 / EDITORIAL BOTTLENECK

Client Approval Latency

Slow client review turnaround times threatening scheduled monthly indexation milestones.

03 / TECHNICAL FIDELITY

Schema & Markup QA

Ensuring writers and developers accurately implement structured FAQ schema, canonicals, and entity links.

Release Verification

My Role & Operational Decisions

1. Standardized Editorial Brief Templates

I built structured content brief templates that eliminated ambiguity for writers: each brief specified exact word-count thresholds, header hierarchy (H1, H2, H3), semantic LSI term targets, and required internal linking destinations.

2. Answer-First AI Overview Formatting

Coordinated with SEO strategists to format landing page headers with direct answers within the opening 40 words, optimizing pages for Google AI Overviews and conversational search engines while maintaining human readability.
