Website Redesign & Launch Coordination

Guiding a complete website overhaul from Figma wireframes to WordPress development, overseeing milestone schedules, staging QA, and zero-downtime production cutovers.

My Role Digital Project Manager & QA Lead
Timeline 4 Months
Team & Stakeholders UI/UX Designers, Web Developers, SEO Lead & Client
Scope & Focus Figma Design Handoff • WordPress CMS Build • Production Launch

Project Overview

Website redesigns are high-stakes initiatives where design aspirations frequently collide with technical constraints, budget parameters, and fixed launch dates.

In this engagement, I led the digital delivery of a comprehensive website overhaul, acting as the primary bridge between UI/UX designers, WordPress developers, technical SEO analysts, and client executives. My focus was orchestrating a smooth transition from initial Figma prototypes into a production-ready, search-optimized web experience.

The Core Challenge

Bridging Design Intent with Engineering Feasibility Under Fixed Timelines

The project faced three critical risks that required structured management intervention:

01 / DESIGN-DEV GAP

Component Discrepancies

Desktop-only Figma mockups lacking mobile responsiveness rules, leading to developer guesswork.

02 / SEO PRESERVATION

Redirect & Indexation Risk

Risk of broken legacy URLs and ranking drop without a strict 1:1 redirect map and canonical tag audit.

03 / CUTOVER DOWNTIME

Launch Deployment Risk

DNS transitions, SSL certification, and hosting migrations requiring seamless coordination to prevent downtime.

Project Process & Milestone Timeline

I structured the 4-month lifecycle into five distinct milestone gates, ensuring no phase commenced until formal criteria were satisfied:

M1 • Weeks 1-3
Discovery & Wireframes

Cross-device testing & SEO checks

M5 • Week 16
Launch & Post-QA

DNS cutover & GSC verification

My Role & Operational Decisions

1. Formal Design Handoff Review

Prior to coding, I facilitated a joint walkthrough between the lead designer and developer in Figma. We verified mobile viewport behaviors, hover transitions, and typography scale, eliminating over 80% of typical mid-sprint redesign questions.

2. Exhaustive Pre-Launch QA Checklist

I developed and managed a 42-point quality assurance protocol spanning mobile responsive rendering, form submission testing, Google Tag Manager event tracking, meta tags, schema markup, and HTTP status code verification.
