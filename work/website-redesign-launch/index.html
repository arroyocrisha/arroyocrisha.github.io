Website Redesign & Launch Coordination

Coordinating designers, developers, and SEO specialists throughout website redesign, quality assurance, and launch preparation.

Role Digital Project Manager — Web Delivery & QA Coordination
Team & Stakeholders Design, Development & SEO Teams
Scope & Focus Design Handoff • Development Coordination • QA & Launch Preparation
01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Website redesign projects require more than translating designs into functional pages. They involve aligning stakeholder expectations, managing dependencies, coordinating revisions, and ensuring that the final website meets design, functionality, and SEO requirements.

Effective delivery depended on maintaining clear communication across teams, tracking deliverables, coordinating feedback, and guiding the transition from design through development and launch.

Illustrative Delivery Architecture Design → Dev → QA
02 / The Coordination Challenge

Keeping design, development, and SEO requirements aligned.

Website projects involve specialists working on different aspects of the same deliverable. Design revisions, development dependencies, and SEO requirements can create competing priorities.

Day-to-day coordination centered on maintaining clarity around requirements, task ownership, stakeholder feedback, and overall project progress.

01 / VISUAL CONSISTENCY

Design Alignment

Translating visual layouts, responsive behavior, and stakeholder revisions into clear specifications for implementation.

02 / TASK SEQUENCING

Development Dependencies

Sequencing content, design assets, and technical tasks so blockers are surfaced and resolved before stalling builds.

03 / LAUNCH STANDARDS

Quality & SEO Requirements

Ensuring page structure, content accuracy, functional elements, and on-page SEO requirements are verified prior to launch.
