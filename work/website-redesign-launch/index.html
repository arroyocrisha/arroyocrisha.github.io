01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Assessing Requirements Before Execution Begins

Website projects vary significantly depending on client goals and starting conditions. Some projects begin entirely from scratch for clients without a digital presence, while others involve existing websites that need targeted visual updates or deeper structural and code changes.

Project ownership also starts at different points—sometimes guiding an initiative from initial intake and requirements planning, and other times stepping into a project already in progress to maintain momentum. In every case, carefully assessing requirements upfront establishes the right project classification and prevents avoidable scope drift during execution.

02 / PROJECT CLASSIFICATIONS

Types of Website Projects

These classifications reflect the actual operational workflow used in day-to-day agency delivery to distinguish project scope before tasks are ticketed and assigned.

TYPE 01 New Build from Scratch

Full Website Development

For clients without an existing website. The project involves building a new website based on agreed requirements.

Scope Focus: Complete site structure, new page designs, content placement, and full development implementation.

TYPE 02 Structural & Experience Overhaul

Website Revamp

For clients with existing websites that require significant changes to their appearance, code, structure, or overall website experience.

Scope Focus: Reworked architecture, updated page structures, code improvements, and refreshed visual direction.

TYPE 03 Visual Refresh • Retained Content

Website Redesign

For clients who want to update the visual appearance of their website while retaining agreed pages, sections, navigation, and existing content.

Scope Focus: Updated visual interface while preserving documented copy, navigation structure, and agreed page elements.

"Define the work before assigning the work."

Assessing requirements early helps establish the appropriate approach, clarify scope, and avoid unnecessary changes during execution.

04 / PM JUDGMENT & SCOPE CONTROL

Project Assessment & Task Validation

Before a designer or developer begins work, incoming requests are evaluated and verified against the original briefing to ensure the ticket accurately reflects the agreed scope.

  1. 1

    Internal Requirements Discussion: The team holds an internal meeting to discuss the client's requirements.

  2. 2

    Classification Assessment: Evaluate the requested work to determine whether it should be classified as website development, redesign, revamp, or recoding.

  3. 3

    Cross-Functional Recommendation: Communicate classification and approach recommendations to the SEO Specialist and Account Manager.

  4. 4

    Platform Ticketing: Once the direction is agreed, the project is ticketed through Monday.com or the relevant internal platform.

  5. 5

    Ticket-to-Discussion Verification: Review the submitted ticket against the original discussion to confirm that requirements are complete and accurate.

  6. 6

    Change vs. Retention Audit: For redesign projects, verify which pages and elements should change and which should remain unchanged.

  7. 7

    Copy & Navigation Documentation: Check that requirements to retain existing website copy and navigation are properly documented before work is assigned.

05 / CONFIRMED OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

Website Design & Handoff Workflow

The eight-stage sequence below reflects the confirmed process from initial internal briefing through final design approval and development handoff.

Stages 01 – 08
STAGE 01 Alignment

Internal Briefing

The team discusses the client's requirements, expected changes, and initial scope.

PM Contribution

Assess the request and identify the appropriate project approach.

STAGE 02 Validation

Ticket Review & Scope Validation

The task is submitted through the project management platform.

PM Contribution

Review requirements for completeness, identify missing details, and verify what should change or remain unchanged.

STAGE 03 Allocation

Designer Assignment

A designer is assigned to begin the website design process.

PM Contribution

Allocate the design task and communicate the agreed scope.

STAGE 04 Concepting

Homepage Design Concepts

The designer typically creates two homepage design concepts.

PM Contribution

Review the concepts against the project requirements and coordinate consideration of SEO principles.

STAGE 05 Client Selection

Client Design Selection

The homepage concepts are submitted for client review.

PM Contribution

Coordinate the review and approval process, including feedback or requested revisions.

STAGE 06 Inner Pages

Remaining Page Designs

Once the homepage direction is approved, the remaining pages within scope are designed using the selected visual direction.

PM Contribution

Coordinate design execution and monitor consistency with the approved homepage concept.

STAGE 07 Sign-Off

Final Design Approval

The completed page designs are submitted for client checking.

PM Contribution

Track feedback, coordinate necessary revisions, and confirm that approval has been received before moving forward.

STAGE 08 Transition

Development Handoff

Once the designs are approved, the project proceeds to development.

PM Contribution

Coordinate the transition from approved designs to development.

Following Stage 08 (Development Handoff): Web developers implement the approved designs while project management continues supporting progress tracking, pre-launch quality reviews across layouts, functionality, content, and SEO requirements, and launch readiness coordination.
