03 / HOW I WORK

The 7-Stage Delivery Workflow

How I coordinate multidisciplinary squads from initial brief to post-launch optimization—without friction.

01 Requirements & Feasibility

Discovery & Alignment

Clarify business goals, extract technical prerequisites, and align cross-functional leads on deliverables before work begins.

02 Timelines & Capacity

Planning & Scoping

Map dependencies, break initiatives into milestone deliverables, negotiate realistic estimates, and allocate team capacity.

03 Design-Dev Handoff

Design Coordination

Facilitate wireframe walkthroughs between UI/UX designers and engineers to ensure components are responsive and feasible.

04 Blocker Resolution

Development Sprints

Coordinate active builds, run daily standups, protect teams from scope creep, and solve cross-system dependencies.

05 Pre-Launch Gate

Quality Assurance

Lead end-to-end staging checks covering responsive rendering, form submissions, technical SEO tags, and tracking.

06 Cutover Execution

Production Launch

Execute launch runbooks, manage DNS and SSL transitions, and verify live environments with zero unplanned downtime.

07 Retrospective & Monitoring

Post-Launch Review

Monitor initial crawl and analytics indexing, resolve early client feedback, and conduct sprint retrospectives.
